## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE beautiful original premium app icon for Vela, a cryptocurrency trading dashboard. Subject: a clearly recognizable elegant WHALE, sculpted from a continuous flowing ribbon, compact rounded body, graceful upward curling tail and small tasteful eye. Whale silhouette is the main focus, not letters. Refined 3D soft sculpture, satin emerald green and luminous mint jade, subtle pale mint highlights, deep forest green shaded underside, sophisticated fintech identity, approachable but not childish. Integrate the tiniest suggestion of an ascending market wave into the tail shape, no separate charts. Balanced bold silhouette readable at 32px, centered square composition, fill about 88% of canvas. No typography, no letters, no text, no coins, no water spout, no ocean scene, no frame, no tile or background, no watermark. Genuinely transparent background around the whale. Single finished production quality icon, not a contact sheet. Fits an existing dark forest green dashboard with mint accents.
